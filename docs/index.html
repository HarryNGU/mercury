What it does

One assistant for everything you do on your Mac.

Ask

Ask about anything on your screen

If you're stuck on something in your notes, a chart in Excel or a paragraph in a PDF, press ⌥\ and ask. Mercury looks at your screen and answers the way a friend would over text, with pictures, videos and links when they help.

  • It only looks at your screen when you ask
  • Diagrams, molecules and videos show up in the chat
  • It works over any app, so there's nothing to copy

Knows you

It already knows you

Mercury works with the AI you already use. Connect Claude and it knows your notes, your calendar and your inbox. If you use ChatGPT, you can bring over what it remembers about you with one paste.

  • Uses your Claude memory and connected apps
  • Brings in what ChatGPT knows about you
  • Kept on your Mac, and you can delete it any time

Do

It writes, you send

Say “reply to Sam and tell him I'll be there at 6” and Mercury writes the reply in Gmail or Outlook, shows it to you, and waits. It only sends once you tap Allow or say “yes”.

  • Drafts in Gmail, Outlook, Messages, Instagram and Messenger
  • Shows an Allow or Deny card before anything is sent
  • Chat messages are typed in for you to send yourself

  • Works in any text box on your Mac
  • Takes out the ums and applies your corrections
  • Your speech is turned into text on your Mac

Listen

Help during calls

Turn on Listen during a Zoom or Google Meet call, a lecture or a video. Mercury hears your Mac's sound (never your microphone) and can suggest what to say, or sum up what you missed.

  • Ask “what should I say?” with one tap
  • Summaries and to do lists from long calls
  • A red dot shows whenever it's listening

Record

Record once, post anywhere

Hover over any window and click to record. Your face sits in a round bubble and the background noise is gone. Then edit it properly in Mercury, or make captioned versions for Reels, TikTok, Shorts and YouTube in one click.

  • Pauses are cut out for you
  • Big captions timed to what you say
  • A full editor with sound clean up, text, zooms and music

Questions

Common questions

Is Mercury really free?

Yes. It's open source, with no paid version and no ads. The assistant uses an AI you bring yourself (your Claude plan or your own API key).

Can Mercury do anything without me knowing?

No. It only looks, listens or does something when you ask. A red dot shows while it's listening, and anything that sends or changes something waits for you to press Allow.

I use ChatGPT. Does Mercury know what it knows about me?

You can bring it over. Mercury gives you a short prompt to paste into ChatGPT, and you paste the answer back. It's saved on your Mac and you can delete it any time. Mercury's answers themselves come from Claude.

Which Macs does it work on?

Any Mac on macOS 15 Sequoia or later, with Apple silicon or Intel. There's no Windows or phone version yet.

Is this the Mercury bank?

No. This Mercury is a free, open source assistant app for the Mac, made by one person. It has nothing to do with any bank or company of the same name.
